# Tally privacy policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

Tally is an Android app that tracks your spending by reading the transaction alerts
your bank sends by SMS. This policy explains what Tally reads, what it keeps, and what
it does not do.

Contact: tinkwarelabs@gmail.com

## The short version

- Tally works entirely on your phone. It has no server and no account.
- Tally cannot use the internet. The app does not request Android's internet permission.
- Nothing Tally reads or stores is sent to us or to anyone else.
- There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- You can delete everything at any time.

## What Tally reads

### SMS messages

If you allow SMS access, Tally reads the SMS messages on your phone to find
transaction alerts from banks, cards, UPI apps and wallets. It reads messages already in
your inbox for the period you choose during setup, and new messages as they arrive.

From a transaction alert Tally takes the amount, the date, the merchant or payee, the
bank name and the last digits of the account or card. It also keeps the text of that
alert so you can check it on the review screen.

One-time passwords, personal messages and promotional messages are skipped. Tally does
not keep them.

SMS access is optional. Without it you can still add expenses by hand. You can turn SMS
reading off in Profile, or remove the permission in Android Settings.

### Contacts

Tally asks for your contacts only if you choose to sync them in the Debts section. It
then copies the names and the first phone number of your contacts into its own storage
on your phone, so you can pick a person when you record a debt or split. Contacts are
not sent anywhere. Contacts access is optional; you can type a name instead.

### Notifications

If you allow notifications, Tally shows an alert when it adds a new expense and, if you
turn it on, a weekly summary.

### Fingerprint or screen lock

If you turn on app lock, Android checks your fingerprint, face or PIN and tells Tally
only whether the check passed. Tally never sees or stores your biometric data.

## Where your data is kept

Everything is stored in a database in Tally's private storage on your phone: your
transactions, the text of the transaction alerts, accounts, categories, budgets, debts,
synced contacts and settings.

If Android's device backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include
Tally's data in that backup. That backup is made and controlled by Android and your
Google account, not by Tally, and we cannot see it.

## What Tally shares

Nothing. We do not collect, sell or share your data, and we have no access to it.

The only time data leaves the app is when you ask for it: "Export transactions (CSV)"
in Profile creates a file and hands it to the app you choose in Android's share sheet.

## Deleting your data

- In the app: Profile -> "Delete all my data" removes everything Tally has stored.
- Uninstalling Tally removes its data from your phone.

## Children

Tally is meant for adults and is not directed at children.
